1. Overview

Join Tour ("Join Tour", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Join Tour mobile application (the "App"), a tour-booking platform that lets travelers discover tours, make escrow-protected bookings, and communicate with tour organizers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the App, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you.

By creating an account or otherwise using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

You sign in with your email address and a one-time code we send to it, or with Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple. The App does not use passwords. When you register or log in, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Your Google or Apple account identifier, if you sign in with Google or Apple (we receive your name and email from that provider, never your Google or Apple password)
  • Profile photo, if you choose to set one
  • Phone number, if you choose to add one
  • The date and version of the Terms you accepted

2.2 Booking Information

When you book a tour, we collect the details needed to fulfill and manage that booking:

  • Full name and phone number of the primary contact for the booking
  • Number of travelers
  • Optional notes or special requests you add to a booking
  • Selected tour, schedule/date, and booking status/history
  • Check-in / booking-pass codes generated for your booking

2.3 Payment Information

Join Tour uses an escrow-protected payment flow: funds you pay are held until the tour is completed and released to the organizer afterward. To support saved payment methods, we store:

  • Cardholder name
  • Card brand (e.g. Visa, Mastercard)
  • Last 4 digits of the card number
  • Card expiration month/year

We do not store your full card number or card security code (CVV) in the App. Where a third-party payment processor is used to handle a transaction, your full card details are transmitted directly to and processed by that provider under its own privacy policy — we only receive and retain the limited details listed above.

2.4 Reviews and Ratings

If you submit a review or star rating for a tour, place, or organizer, we collect and publicly display that content, including any photos you attach, along with your display name.

2.4.1 Travel Map, Memories, and Goals

If you use the map, we store the places you mark as visited or as places you want to go, and the short notes ("memories") you save about places you have visited. This information is linked to your account and is not shown to other users. The yearly travel goal you set is kept only on your device.

2.5 Messages, Photos, and Voice Messages

The App includes chats between travelers and tour organizers, including group chats for a tour. When you use them, we collect and store the messages you send, the photos you attach, and the voice messages you record, so that they can be delivered to the other members of the chat. We also keep the reports you submit about other users or content, and the list of users you have blocked.

2.6 Push Notifications and Device Data

To deliver booking, payment, and organizer updates via push notification, we collect:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token
  • Device identifier and platform (iOS/Android)
  • App version
  • Notification content and read/unread status

Push notifications are delivered using Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service. See Firebase's privacy information for details on how Firebase processes this data on our behalf.

2.7 Usage and Log Data

Our servers automatically log standard technical data when you use the App, such as IP address, request timestamps, app version, and device/OS information, for security, debugging, and service-reliability purposes.

2.8 Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used, for example which screens are opened and which features are used. It collects app interaction events together with an app-instance identifier and device information. We use this data only in aggregate to improve the App, and not for advertising. See Firebase's privacy information for details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • To process, confirm, and manage tour bookings and escrow-protected payments
  • To generate booking/check-in passes and facilitate check-in with organizers
  • To let you save and manage payment methods for future bookings
  • To display reviews and ratings to other users
  • To deliver chat messages, photos, and voice messages to the other members of a chat
  • To review reports, filter abusive content, and block or suspend accounts that break our Terms
  • To send booking, payment, and account-related push notifications
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the App is used
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Tour organizers: booking contact details (name, phone number, traveler count, notes) are shared with the organizer of a tour you book, so they can fulfill your booking.
  • Other users: your display name, avatar, and any reviews/ratings you submit are visible to other users of the App. Messages, photos, and voice messages you send in a chat are visible to the members of that chat.
  • Service providers: we use infrastructure and processing providers that process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations: cloud hosting and file storage, an email delivery provider for sign-in codes and account emails, Google Firebase for push notifications and analytics, and Google and Apple for sign-in.
  • Payment processors: if a transaction is routed through a third-party payment processor, your payment details are shared with that processor to complete the transaction.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Join Tour, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers: information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy.

5. Device Permissions

The App asks for each of these permissions only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Push notifications: to deliver booking and account alerts.
  • Camera: to take a profile photo.
  • Photo library: to choose a profile photo, or photos for a review or a chat message.
  • Microphone: to record a voice message in a chat.

You can turn any of these off at any time in your device's system settings. The App does not request access to your device's location or contacts.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account, booking, and payment-method metadata for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to resolve disputes, comply with legal and accounting obligations, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, what is removed and what is kept is described on our account deletion page.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted network transport (HTTPS/TLS), to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and update your profile information from within the App.
  • Payment methods: you can add, edit, or delete saved payment methods at any time from the App.
  • Account deletion: you can delete your account in the App (Profile → Delete account), or request deletion by email. See our account deletion page for the steps and what data is removed.
  • Blocking and reporting: you can block other users and report users or content from within the App.
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Regional rights: depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA, including the right to access, delete, correct, or port your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your own.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
