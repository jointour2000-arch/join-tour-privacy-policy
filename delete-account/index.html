Join Tour — Delete Your Account

Applies to the Join Tour iOS and Android application, published by Join Tour

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Join Tour app and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete account again.

Your account is deleted immediately, and we send a confirmation email to the address the account used.

If you have an upcoming trip that can still be cancelled, the app asks you to cancel it first. Organizer accounts can't be deleted in the app. Request deletion by email instead.

Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, email us from the address you use for Join Tour:

  • To: jointour2000@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my Join Tour account

We may ask you to confirm that you own the account. We delete it within 30 days of your request and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your profile photo
  • The link to your Google or Apple sign-in
  • Your saved payment cards
  • Your saved tours (wishlist)
  • Your devices' push notification tokens
  • Your membership in every chat. You are removed from all chats and signed out on all devices.

What is kept

Some records stay after deletion, but they are no longer linked to your name, email, or phone number. They appear as "Deleted User".

DataWhy it is kept
Bookings and payment records Needed by the tour organizer, for refunds and disputes, and for our legal and accounting obligations. Kept for as long as those obligations require.
Chat messages, photos, and voice messages you already sent They are part of a conversation other members still see.
Reviews and ratings you published They stay visible as "Deleted User". Email us if you want them removed.
Server logs Kept for a limited period for security and to prevent abuse.

You can create a new account later with the same email address. It starts empty. Nothing from your deleted account is restored. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
